## Project Concept

### What are you building?

I am building a professional, responsive portfolio website template to showcase my work in Multimedia & Graphic Design. The site features a "Modern Editorial" aesthetic with fluid animations, a bilingual interface (English/Spanish), and dynamic project case studies.

### Who is it for?

The target audience includes Creative Directors, HR Recruiters, Design Studios, and potential clients looking for talent in Branding, Editorial Design, and UX/UI.

### Why does it matter?

This project serves as the central hub for my professional identity, demonstrating not just my design outputs but my ability to implement them technically. It provides a reusable, high-performance foundation (template) that allows me to present my case studies (like Applaws and La Llorona) with a custom narrative flow rather than relying on generic platforms like Behance.

## Design Direction

### Visual Style

The design follows a Modern Editorial / Swiss Style aesthetic. It relies on high contrast, "Oversized" typography, and negative space to create impact. The interface features micro-interactions (custom cursor) to add a layer of sophistication and "delight."

### Color Palette

Primary: Electric Blue #0903ca

Background: Off-White #f2f2f2

Text: Dark Grey/Black #141414

### Typography

Headings (Display): Instrument Serif – Used for large, impact titles to give an elegant, editorial feel.

Body/UI: Helvetica Neue – Used for navigation, buttons, and long-form text for readability and neutrality.

## Reflection Questions

### What excites you most about this project?

I am most excited about the integration of GSAP animations and the custom cursor logic. Seeing the static design come to life with scroll-triggered movements makes the user experience feel immersive and high-end, distinguishing it from a standard PDF portfolio.

### What challenges do you anticipate?

The main challenge was organizing the JavaScript code. Moving from a monolithic script to a modular architecture (separating Translations, UI, and Animations) was difficult but necessary for maintainability. Additionally, ensuring the "Fluid Typography" looked perfect on all screen sizes required precise tuning of clamp() values.

### How does this project connect to your learning goals?

## This project bridges the gap between my design creativity and technical execution. It validates my ability to build what I design, giving me a competitive edge in UX/UI roles where understanding frontend constraints (like semantic HTML and responsive grids) is crucial
